# 03 — Delivery Playbook: The Weekly Workflow

This is the SOP. What you do for each client, every week, every month. AI-augmented throughout.

---

## 1. Client Onboarding (Week 1, ~3 hours per client)

### Step 1: Contract + Payment (15 min)

- Client signs service agreement via Stripe payment link
- Setup fee ($200-500, waived for first 3 pilots) charged immediately
- First month of recurring fee charged on signing
- Send welcome email with "What to Expect in Your First 30 Days" doc
- Add to client tracker (Google Sheet — `clients`, `start_date`, `tier`, `niche`, `monthly_fee`, `GBP_url`, `contact_name`, `contact_email`, `last_post_date`, `last_review_response`, `last_report_date`, `churn_risk`)

### Step 2: Access Request (5 min)

- Email template: "Please add me as a Manager on your Google Business Profile. Here's how: [link to Google's instructions]. Reply when done."
- Wait for confirmation
- Verify access in GBP dashboard

### Step 3: Discovery Call (30 min, Zoom)

Use this script:

> "Thanks for taking 30 minutes. Goal of this call: I need to understand your business well enough to make your Google listing sound like *you* wrote it.
>
> Three questions:
> 1. **What's your specialty?** If I Google '[your service] in [city],' what do you want to be known for?
> 2. **Who is your ideal customer?** Be specific — age, situation, what brings them in.
> 3. **What makes you different from the 3 other [your service] businesses within a mile of you?**
>
> Then: Walk me through your typical week — when are you busiest, when are you slow? Any seasonal patterns? Any promotions coming up?
>
> Last: Send me 10-15 photos — your office, your team, your work, your customers (with permission). I'll rotate these into your posts."

### Step 4: Initial Audit + Optimization (90 min)

**The full GBP audit checklist (deliver to client on Day 7):**

- [ ] Claim/verify status
- [ ] Business name (must be exact legal name, no keyword stuffing)
- [ ] Address (matches website, citations)
- [ ] Phone (local number, not tracking)
- [ ] Website URL (working, loads fast)
- [ ] Hours (regular + special hours for holidays)
- [ ] Primary category (most important for ranking)
- [ ] 4-5 secondary categories
- [ ] Business description (600 chars, primary + secondary keywords, voice)
- [ ] Opening date
- [ ] Service areas (cities/towns)
- [ ] Services (list each service with description + price if applicable)
- [ ] Attributes (woman-owned, wheelchair accessible, free parking, etc.)
- [ ] Photos: logo, cover, exterior, interior, team, work samples, before/afters
- [ ] Q&A seed (5-10 common questions with answers)
- [ ] Messaging enabled (if owner wants it)
- [ ] Booking/appointment link
- [ ] Posts: 1 "welcome" post on Day 1
- [ ] Reviews: audit current reviews, flag any needing response

**Tools:**
- GBP dashboard for the edits
- Localith for bulk if multi-location
- BrightLocal free audit tool for the audit report PDF
- AI to draft the description and Q&A

### Step 5: Day 7 — First Deliverable

Send a "First Week Recap" email:

```
Hi [Name],

Week 1 complete. Here's what I did:

✓ Audited your GBP (current score vs. top 3 competitors — attached)
✓ Optimized your profile (description, categories, services, photos)
✓ Seeded 8 common Q&As
✓ Posted your first Google Post ("Welcome to [Business]...")
✓ Responded to 3 new reviews

Your first monthly report will arrive on [date]. If you have any questions
or want to send me more photos, just reply to this email.

[Your name]
```

Attach the audit report PDF. **This is the "wow" moment that locks in retention.**

## 2. The Weekly Workflow (30-45 min per client)

### Monday: Review + Plan (10 min per client)

- Check GBP for new reviews, questions, messages
- Check Insights for any anomalies (huge spike, big drop)
- Review next 2 weeks of post calendar
- Move 5-10 min per client — batch across all clients

### Tuesday: Content Drafting (15 min per client)

- Draft next week's Google Post (use AI prompt in `06-AI-PROMPTS.md`)
- Draft responses to all new reviews (use AI prompt, personalize 1-2)
- Draft any Q&A responses
- Batch with all clients — 2-3 hours every Tuesday for all clients

### Wednesday/Thursday: Approval + Scheduling (10 min per client)

- Review AI drafts, edit for accuracy/voice
- Upload photos, schedule posts
- Approve review responses, post them
- Batch with all clients

### Friday: Client Communication (10 min per client, batched)

- Send Loom updates to active clients (only for those with new reviews or significant changes — don't spam)
- Email check, respond to client questions
- Plan next week

## 3. The Monthly Workflow (~1 hour per client)

### Tasks

1. **Pull GBP Insights** (5 min per client)
   - Searches (direct + discovery)
   - Views (search + maps)
   - Calls (total + by time of day)
   - Direction requests
   - Website clicks
   - Photo views
   - Bookings (if enabled)
   - Compare to last month

2. **Generate monthly report** (15 min per client, AI-assisted)
   - Use the template in `06-AI-PROMPTS.md`
   - Include: highlights, metrics vs. last month, reviews this month, top posts, recommendations for next month
   - Export as PDF or share as a Notion/Google Doc

3. **Send to client + schedule call** (5 min per client)
   - Email report 2 days before call
   - Ask them to review and bring questions
   - 30-min call: walk through report, get their input, plan next month

4. **Quarterly deep-dive** (2 hours per client, only on Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 end)
   - Competitor analysis (top 3 in their area)
   - Review velocity, photo count, post frequency vs. them
   - Citation audit (BrightLocal)
   - Strategy call: where to invest next quarter

### What "good" looks like per client per month

- 4 Google Posts published on schedule
- All reviews responded to within 24 hours
- 2-3 new photos uploaded
- All new Q&As answered
- 1 monthly report sent + 1 call completed
- Client feels like you know their business

### What "bad" looks like (churn signals)

- Missed posts 2+ weeks in a row
- Reviews piling up unanswered
- Client doesn't return your call for the monthly review
- Owner complaining about lack of results (without ever looking at the report)
- Owner asks you to "just do SEO" or "run our ads" (scope creep → set boundary or exit)

## 4. Quality Control Checklist (run monthly, ~30 min across all clients)

- [ ] Every client has a post in the last 7 days
- [ ] No client has unanswered reviews >48 hours
- [ ] Every client got their monthly report this month
- [ ] Every client had their monthly call this month
- [ ] No client is in "warning" status (post skipped + review unresponded)
- [ ] All access credentials are valid (no removed managers)
- [ ] Stripe payments all processed cleanly
- [ ] All client files in Google Drive are organized
- [ ] No client is >60 days without a touchpoint

If any box fails, fix it Monday morning.

## 5. The "Boring Work" Optimization

Most of the work becomes repetitive fast. Optimize aggressively:

### Templates to build in week 1 (one-time)

- Welcome email
- Access request email
- Discovery call script
- Initial audit template (PDF)
- First-week recap email
- Weekly client update email
- Monthly report template
- Quarterly review template
- Renewal/check-in email
- Cancellation flow (3-step: identify, save, exit)

### AI prompts to build in week 1

- Review response generator (in `06-AI-PROMPTS.md`)
- Google Post copy generator
- Monthly report generator
- Audit Loom script generator
- Q&A seed generator

### Batch everything

- All client posts drafted in 1 sitting (Tuesday morning)
- All review responses drafted in 1 sitting (Tuesday afternoon)
- All monthly reports generated in 1 sitting (last Friday of month)
- All quarterly reviews done in 1 week (first week of quarter)

**The goal: 80% of work done in 2-3 focused sessions per week, not spread across 7 days.**

## 6. The First 90 Days Client Lifecycle

### Day 0: Sign + Pay
### Day 1-3: Welcome + access + discovery
### Day 7: First deliverables (audit, first post, first review responses)
### Day 14: Check-in email ("How's it going? Any questions?")
### Day 30: First monthly report + first call
### Day 60: Second monthly report + check-in
### Day 90: Quarterly review + case study request ("Mind if I share your results?")
### Day 180: Half-year check-in + offer Pro tier or upsell
### Day 270: Renewal check-in (always 90 days before they might leave)
### Day 365: Annual review + price increase conversation

### Renewal tactics

- 90 days before renewal: "Hey, just a heads up that we'll be sending your annual review in [month]. Want to schedule a 30-min strategy call to align on next year?"
- Annual review: show before/after metrics, ask "what do you want to focus on next year?"
- Price increase: "We're raising rates to $X for new clients starting [date]. As a current client, your rate stays at $Y for another 12 months. Want to lock that in now with annual prepay and save 10%?"

Annual prepay with 10% discount is your best churn-killer.

## 7. What Goes Wrong (and How to Fix It)

### Common Issue 1: "I'm not seeing results"
**Diagnosis:** Either you haven't given it enough time (60-90 days for any meaningful signal) or the listing was already in good shape.
**Fix:** Pull the GBP Insights data. Show views, calls, direction requests over time. If they're up, you have leverage. If flat, do a deeper competitor analysis and find the actual gap (photos? reviews? categories?).

### Common Issue 2: "I want you to also do my [X]"
**Diagnosis:** Scope creep.
**Fix:** "I can definitely refer you to someone who does that. My focus is GBP and local SEO — if I start doing everything, I won't be great at anything. Want me to make an intro?"

### Common Issue 3: "Can you do this for free for a month to test?"
**Diagnosis:** Price-sensitive or doesn't trust you yet.
**Fix:** "I offer a free audit Loom so you can see what I'd do without any commitment. After that, the work starts at $500/mo. If you're not seeing value after 90 days, I'll work free until you do. Fair?"

### Common Issue 4: "I got a 1-star review from a crazy person"
**Diagnosis:** Emotional owner, needs a hand-hold.
**Fix:** Respond within 1 hour with a public, professional response (use the template in `06-AI-PROMPTS.md`). Then a private email: "Hey, saw the review. Here's what I responded with. I know it's frustrating — most prospects will read the response, not the review. We'll get past this. Want me to also help you generate more 5-star reviews to dilute the impact?"

### Common Issue 5: Owner stops responding to your updates
**Diagnosis:** Either disengaged, considering leaving, or busy season hit.
**Fix:** Send a "checking in" email. If no response in 7 days, a phone call. If still nothing, pause active work, send monthly report anyway. If 30 days no response, send a "should we wrap this up?" email. Don't work for clients who don't engage.

## 8. The "AI Handles 70%" Reality

The biggest time-saver: AI does the actual writing, you do the actual *judgment*.

**AI does:**
- Review response drafts
- Post copy drafts
- Monthly report content
- Audit Loom scripts
- Q&A seed generation
- Photo caption suggestions

**You do:**
- Review AI outputs for tone, accuracy, brand fit (10-20%)
- Final approval before publishing
- All client communication
- Sales + discovery
- Strategy + optimization decisions

The split: 70% AI draft, 20% you edit, 10% you publish. **Your actual time per client per week after onboarding: 20-30 minutes.**

The 30 minutes is dominated by:
1. Reviewing AI drafts (10 min)
2. Personalizing for the specific client (5 min)
3. Quality control + scheduling (5 min)
4. Communicating with the client (5 min)

This is what makes the business scalable. Without AI, you'd need 1-2 hours per client per week. With AI: 30 minutes.
