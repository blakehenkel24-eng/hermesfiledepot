### Day -5 (Thursday): Cold Email Setup

- [ ] **Apollo** — build prospect list: 50 dental practices in Schaumburg + 10-mile radius
- [ ] **Write cold email sequence** — use template in `04-SALES-PLAYBOOK.md`, personalize to dental

### Day -4 (Friday): Materials
- [ ] **Print 50 business cards** — Vistaprint, 2-day shipping

### Day -1 (Monday): Final Prep
- [ ] **Test the entire flow:** Visit your own Calendly. Try to book. Make sure Stripe link works. Make sure your site is live.

- [ ] **Charge your phone, clear your calendar for tomorrow's walk-ins.**

## Week 1: First Outreach (Days 1-7)

### Day 1 (Tuesday): First Walk-Ins
**Goal:** 5 walk-ins. Don't worry about closing.
- [ ] **Pick a 2-square-mile zone** with 10+ target businesses. Drive there.
- [ ] **5 walk-ins, 10am-1pm.** Use the script in `04-SALES-PLAYBOOK.md`.
- [ ] **Drop 1-pager + business card** at each one.
- [ ] **Send follow-up email** to anyone who said yes to the audit (within 4 hours of walk-in).
- [ ] **End of day:** Log all 5 walk-ins in your prospect tracker (Google Sheet). Note: name, business, contact, what they said, next step.

### Day 2 (Wednesday): Cold Email + First Audit
- [ ] **Send 30 cold emails** (Apollo list, dental niche). Use the 3-touch sequence.
- [ ] **Deliver first Loom audit** to anyone who requested one (1-2 expected).
- [ ] **15 min:** Review cold email open/reply rates. Adjust subject line if needed.

### Day 3 (Thursday): More Walk-Ins
- [ ] **5 more walk-ins** in a different zone.
- [ ] **Send 30 more cold emails.**
- [ ] **Respond to any audit requests** from yesterday.

### Day 4 (Friday): Loom Audit Production
- [ ] **Audit videos backlog:** You should have 1-3 requests. Knock them all out today.
- [ ] **30 cold emails.**
- [ ] **15 min:** Research your top 5 competitors in your niche. What's their offer? Pricing? Positioning?

### Day 5-6 (Weekend): Light Work + Rest
- [ ] **Saturday morning (2-3 hrs):** Walk-ins if you're up for it. Or just prospecting from home.
- [ ] **Sunday: OFF.** Non-negotiable.

### Day 7 (Monday): End of Week 1 Review
- [ ] **Pipeline check:** How many leads? How many engaged? How many audits sent?
- [ ] **What worked:** Double down on it.
- [ ] **What didn't:** Diagnose. Adjust.
- [ ] **Week 2 plan:** Same or adjusted.

**End of Week 1 target:**
- 10-15 walk-ins completed
- 60-90 cold emails sent
- 2-3 audits delivered
- 1-2 discovery calls booked (if lucky)

- [ ] How many hours did I work? (Target: 35-50)

**If you're below 50% of target:** Stop. Read `07-RISK-AND-PIVOT.md`. Consider a pivot.

## Month 3 (Days 61-90): Validation Point

### Goals
- 7-9 active clients
- $3,500-4,500 MRR
- 2-3 new clients this month
- Operational rhythm: <30 min/client/week delivery time
- Decision point: continue, pivot, or stop

### The "Month 3 Decision"

You should now have enough data to know if this business is for you. Ask:

1. **Am I enjoying the work?** (Not just tolerating it.)
2. **Are clients getting results?** (Any case study wins?)
3. **Is the close rate improving?** (Should be 20%+ on audits by now.)
4. **Is MRR growing?** (At least $500-1K/month growth.)
5. **Do I see a path to $10K MRR in 12 months?** (If you can't see it, the model is wrong for you.)

If 4/5 are yes: keep going.
If 3/5 are yes: continue with one specific change (niche, channel, or pricing).
If 2/5 or fewer: pivot or stop.

## The "First Client" Playbook (Day 1, when you sign someone)

The moment the Stripe payment hits, you have 24 hours to:

1. **Send the welcome email** (template in `05-CONTRACTS-AND-TEMPLATES.md`)
2. **Send the access request** (how to add you as GBP manager)
3. **Schedule the discovery call** (Calendly)
4. **Add them to the client tracker**
5. **Celebrate.** This is real. You have a business.

Then in week 1:
- Discovery call (30 min)
- Get GBP access
- Request photos + assets
- Start the audit (90 min)
- First post + first review responses (Day 7)
- Send first-week recap email

**The first client is the hardest. After that, you have a system.**

---

## The "I'm Stuck" Escape Hatches

If at any point in the first 90 days you feel stuck:

1. **Re-read the executive summary.** 5 minutes. Reconnect with why.
2. **Read the case studies of similar businesses.** Find someone 6-12 months ahead of you doing this. See what they did.
3. **Talk to a paying client.** Even if just to hear "yes, the work is helping." That re-energizes.
4. **Adjust the niche.** Don't keep hitting a wall in dental if medspa is more receptive.
5. **Adjust the offer.** If $500 isn't closing, try $300. If $300 is too low, try $1,000.
6. **Take a Sunday.** The answer often comes when you stop trying.

## The Final Pep Talk

You're 23, at a PE firm, with strong business skills and AI fluency. You're considering building a service business on the side. That's already impressive — most people never even consider it.

The reality of the first 90 days: it'll be 60% frustration, 30% uncertainty, and 10% pure joy when you close that first client. The joy scales. The frustration doesn't (much).

By month 6, you'll have a system. By month 12, you'll have an asset. By month 24, you'll have options.

**Start Day 1. Stop reading. Go.**
