# Local Lead Engine — Executive Summary

**The business:** A productized Google Business Profile (GBP) management + local SEO service for professional service businesses (dental, medspa, chiropractic, HVAC, legal, real estate) in affluent Chicago suburbs. AI-augmented delivery, founder-led sales, VA-scaled operations.

**The wedge:** Most local businesses have a neglected Google listing. The businesses that *do* invest use expensive enterprise tools ($500+/mo) or generalist agencies that treat GBP as a side dish. There's a clear gap for a focused, mid-priced specialist ($300-1,000/mo) that does GBP *really well*.

**The founder advantage (Blake):**
- 23, on the Inspire11 PE services team — instant credibility with B2B prospects
- Schaumburg/Chicago-located, access to high-density, high-income service-business corridors
- Strong business acumen, can talk ROI with owners
- Already has Gemini/Claude/ChatGPT premium for AI-augmented delivery
- 1.5hr daily train commute = 2 free hours/day for client work

**The model:**
- $500/mo per client (default Growth tier)
- 30-45 min/week per client after onboarding
- AI handles ~70% of execution (review responses, post copy, audit reports)
- Break-even at 1 client; meaningful income ($5K/mo) at 10 clients
- Scale to $15-30K/mo within 12 months with 1-2 part-time VAs

**Year-1 target:** 15-20 clients = $7,500-10,000 MRR, run on the side of Inspire11.

**Year-2 options:** (a) productize into a micro-SaaS, (b) raise prices and stay small/lean, (c) sell the client book as an agency acquisition, (d) take the cash flow to acquire a boring local business.

## 🧭 The 5-minute version (if you only read one section)

**Why this works in 2026 specifically:**
1. Google's local "3-pack" is the highest-converting real estate on the internet — 42% of local searches result in a map click
2. GBP used to be a "set and forget" listing. Now it's a content platform (posts, photos, Q&A, products, services) that needs weekly care
3. AI makes the care cheap. The owner can do it themselves but won't. You can do it for them, faster.
4. 80% of GBP agencies target enterprise ($1K+/mo pricing, 50+ location clients). The SMB market ($300-1K/mo, 1-3 locations) is wide open

**The 3 numbers that matter:**
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC): ~$50-200 per client (mostly time, not dollars)
- Lifetime value (LTV) per client: $6,000+ (12+ months at $500/mo, retention 80%+)
- LTV/CAC: 30-120x — the business is cash-flow positive from client #1

**The unfair advantage:** Most of your competition is either (a) generalist marketing agencies who treat GBP as a checkbox, or (b) solo operators charging $100/mo. You sit in the middle: specialist quality, mid-tier price, AI-augmented margin.

**The risk most people miss:** The work is repetitive. If you hate answering "did you see that new review I got?" 5 times a day, you won't last 90 days. The business is a *relationships* business more than a *marketing* business after month 3.
