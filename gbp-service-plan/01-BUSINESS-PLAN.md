# 01 — The Business Plan: Local Lead Engine

**Productized Google Business Profile management + local SEO for professional service SMBs in the Chicago metro.**

---

## Part 1: The Market

### The opportunity

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business / GMB) is the single highest-leverage marketing asset for any local service business. The math:

- 46% of all Google searches have local intent
- 76% of "near me" mobile searches result in a store visit within 24 hours
- 88% of consumers trust Google reviews as much as personal recommendations
- The Google "3-pack" (the 3 businesses shown on the map) captures ~42% of clicks; everything else fights for scraps

Yet the average local business GBP is *terrible*. Common failures:
- Unclaimed or unverified (no one owns it)
- Wrong hours, missing categories, no photos
- 2-3 reviews vs. competitor's 80
- No posts, no Q&A, no services listed
- Last updated 2+ years ago

This is a massive, persistent gap. Google keeps adding GBP features (posts, products, services, Q&A, attributes, messaging) faster than small business owners can keep up.

### Why now (2026 specifically)

Three converging trends make this the right moment:

1. **AI makes the work 5-10x faster.** In 2024, this kind of service required 1-2 hours per client per week. In 2026, AI drafts 80% of the work — review responses, post copy, monthly reports, audit deliverables. Same quality, 1/5 the time.

2. **Post-COVID local recovery is uneven.** Many service businesses are busy but their marketing infrastructure is still stuck in 2019. They're not losing customers because of a recession — they're losing them to competitors with better Google listings.

3. **Google is rewarding active GBP management more aggressively.** Their 2025-2026 algorithm updates explicitly favor businesses posting weekly, uploading photos, responding to reviews, and managing Q&A. The "set and forget" GBP is now actively *demoted* in many categories.

### Why this is a wedge (not a saturated market)

Most "local SEO agency" coverage focuses on the wrong end of the market:
- **Enterprise ($1,500-10K/mo):** Birdeye, Yotpo, Chatmeter. Multi-location brands. 6-month sales cycles. Outside the solo founder's reach.
- **DIY tools ($29-99/mo):** Localith, BrightLocal. The business owner has to do the work. Most don't.
- **Generalist agencies ($500-2K/mo):** Do "marketing" — SEO, ads, social. Treat GBP as one of 10 things. Do it poorly.

The gap: **a focused specialist doing GBP + local SEO only, at $300-1,000/mo, for 1-3 location service businesses.** This is the niche. It's also exactly the kind of business a 23-year-old with strong AI tool fluency can dominate.

### The market size (Chicago metro, Schaumburg focus)

- Schaumburg + adjacent suburbs (Hoffman Estates, Elk Grove Village, Rolling Meadows, Arlington Heights, Palatine, Schaumburg, Streamwood, Roselle): ~600K population, ~25K businesses, ~3,500 professional service businesses (rough estimate)
- Of those, ~500-1,000 are the right ICP (dental, medspa, chiro, HVAC, attorney, real estate)
- Average customer LTV: $500-5K+ per service engagement
- Average customer acquisition cost for the business: $50-300
- Realistic share goal: 1-3% of ICP = 5-30 clients in Year 1
- Total addressable market for THIS business: $1-30M GMV in Chicago metro over 3 years

You don't need 1% of the market. You need 30 clients and you're at $180K ARR.

## Part 2: The Offer

### The productized service

Three tiers, identical positioning, different depths:

| Tier | Price | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $300/mo | Newer businesses, owners who want to "test" you before committing. Usually a 3-month intro. |
| **Growth** ⭐ | $500/mo | Default. Most close-able prospects land here. Best balance of value capture and accessibility. |
| **Pro** | $1,000/mo | Established businesses ($1M+ revenue), multi-location, or owners who want the full monty + strategy calls. |

**Default pitch:** "Most of my clients start on the Growth package at $500/mo — weekly posts, every review responded to, photos uploaded, monthly report. If you're running a larger operation and want a strategy call and review generation, the Pro tier is $1,000/mo. Want me to send you a sample audit of your listing so you can see what I'd do in month one?"

### What's included at each tier

**Starter ($300/mo):**
- Initial GBP audit + optimization (one-time)
- 2 Google Posts per month
- Review response (every review, AI-drafted, you approve)
- Monthly summary report
- Email support

**Growth ($500/mo):** Everything in Starter, plus
- 4 Google Posts per month (weekly cadence)
- Photo upload (2-3 per month, from owner's supply or stock)
- Q&A management
- Quarterly competitor analysis
- 30-min monthly call (or Loom if client prefers)
- Priority email support

**Pro ($1,000/mo):** Everything in Growth, plus
- 8 Google Posts per month (twice weekly)
- 4-6 photos per month
- Citation cleanup + 2 new citations/month (Yelp, BBB, Apple Maps, industry directories)
- Review generation campaign (email/SMS to past customers)
- Biweekly 30-min strategy call
- Local SEO optimization (on-page, schema markup for service pages)
- Same-day support SLA

### What's NOT included (refer or trade out)

- Google Ads management → refer to partner, take 10% referral fee
- Website design/development → refer to partner
- Social media management (Instagram/Facebook) → out of scope, refer out
- Reputation beyond Google (Yelp, Facebook) → can add for $100/mo add-on

### The contract

- **Month-to-month**, no annual lock-in
- **30-day termination notice** required from either side
- **Setup fee:** $200-500 one-time for initial audit/optimization (waived for first 3 pilot clients)
- **Payment:** Stripe auto-debit on the 1st of the month
- **Late payment:** 7-day grace, then 1.5%/mo interest
- **Service guarantee:** If we don't deliver a measurable lift in GBP calls/direction requests within 90 days, we work free until you see results (defined: 25% lift in profile views OR 10% lift in calls, whichever comes first)

This guarantee is *cheap* because it almost never triggers — most clients see lift in 30-60 days. But it's a *massive* sales weapon. It removes the only real objection: "Will this actually work?"

### Pricing rationale

- **$500/mo is the sweet spot** because:
  - Low enough that the business owner can approve on a credit card without involving a partner/CFO
  - High enough that 10-15 clients = meaningful income ($5-7.5K/mo)
  - High enough to filter out non-serious prospects
  - Low enough that the per-month ROI is obvious (one new customer pays for 6-12 months)
- **Don't compete on price.** If a prospect says "your competitor does it for $150/mo," thank them and move on. Cheap clients are nightmares.
- **Annual prepay option:** 10% discount ($5,400 vs. $6,000) — cash flow boost + lower churn

## Part 3: The Business Model

### Unit economics (per client, $500/mo Growth tier)

| | $/mo |
|---|---|
| Revenue | $500 |
| Variable cost (AI tools pro-rated) | ~$5 |
| Direct labor (1 hr/mo founder) | ~$50 (opp cost, not cash) |
| **Gross margin** | **$445 (89%)** |
| Customer acquisition cost (founder time amortized) | ~$30 |
| **Net contribution per client** | **~$415/mo** |

At 10 clients, MRR is $5,000. Tools cost ~$100/mo. Effective hourly rate: $5,000 / 12 hours = **$416/hr.**

At 20 clients (with 1 VA), MRR is $10,000. Tools + VA = $2,000/mo. Net: $8,000/mo. Effective rate: ~$300/hr.

This is a margin business. The work is mostly *judgment* (what to post, how to respond to a 1-star review) which doesn't scale linearly. The unit economics don't degrade much as you add clients — you just get more efficient.

### The cash flow

- **Inflow:** Stripe auto-debit, 1st of the month
- **Outflow:** Tools ($50-200/mo), VA wages (when hired), Stripe fees (~3%)
- **Net cash conversion:** 100% (no inventory, no COGS, no A/R)

The business is essentially a recurring-revenue software business without the software.

### Why this is a good first business

1. **Low capital requirement.** Under $500 to start (domain + tools + business cards).
2. **No employees required for Year 1.** You can run 15 clients solo, evenings + weekends.
3. **Recurring revenue from month 1.** Stripe subscriptions.
4. **High gross margin.** 85-90% once established.
5. **No inventory, no shipping, no regulated industry.** Just services.
6. **Defensible through relationships.** Clients stay because they like working with you, not because of switching costs.
7. **Builds leverage.** Once you have 20 clients, you can hire VAs to do 80% of execution. Your time becomes sales + relationships.
8. **Exits well.** An agency book of 20-50 clients at $500-1,000/mo is a sellable asset (3-5x annual revenue = $360K-1.8M exit).

### Why this is also a dangerous first business

1. **It's not sexy.** You'll have friends building AI startups. You will be writing Google posts for dentists. The ego tax is real.
2. **It depends on you.** Until you systematize + hire, you are the business. Vacation = no revenue.
3. **It can feel like a job, not a business.** If you don't systematize, it stays 1:1 time-for-dollars.
4. **Local markets are local.** You can't 10x by going viral. Growth is linear.
5. **Review response is emotionally draining.** Negative reviews are personal to the business owner. They'll be personal to you.

Mitigation is in the operations playbook (`03-DELIVERY-PLAYBOOK.md`).

## Part 4: The Go-to-Market

### ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

**Primary:** Professional service businesses in affluent Chicago suburbs, 1-3 locations, $500K-5M annual revenue, 1-10 employees.

**Best sub-niches (ranked by close rate × deal value):**

1. **Dental practices** — $1-5M revenue, owner-operators, always want more patients, easy to find, high LTV patients. **Start here.**
2. **Medspas / aesthetics** — high LTV customers, owner is typically 30-50, marketing-aware, willing to spend. Excellent fit.
3. **Chiropractors / physical therapy** — similar to dental, often owner-operated, very local.
4. **Personal injury attorneys** — $5-30M revenue, marketing budget is real, high LTV cases. **Higher value but harder close.**
5. **HVAC / plumbing / roofing** — typically multi-trade or multi-truck, owner too busy to manage GBP, will pay if you make it easy.
6. **Real estate agents / teams** — high volume, brand-conscious, want online presence. Hit-or-miss on quality of fit.

**Skip:** Restaurants, retail (high churn, low LTV), B2B SaaS (different buying motion), e-commerce (not local enough).

### Channels (ranked by effectiveness for solo founder in Year 1)

**Channel 1: Door-to-door walk-ins (Channel #1 for first 3-5 clients)**
- 2-3 zip codes with right density
- 5-10 walk-ins per day, 2 days/week
- 40-80 walk-ins/month
- Close rate: 5-15% on free audit follow-up
- Expected: 2-4 new clients/month
- Cost: $0 (gas + business cards)

**Channel 2: Cold email (Channel #1 for scaling past 5 clients)**
- Use Apollo.io free tier for email scraping
- Localith or local SEO tool for finding businesses
- Instantly.ai for sending at scale
- Loom for personalized audit videos
- Expected: 1-3% reply rate, 10-30% close on replies
- 100 emails/day → 3 clients/month at scale

**Channel 3: Referral network (compounds over 6-12 months)**
- $100 Amazon gift card per referral that converts
- LinkedIn posts showing case studies
- Local Chamber / BNI involvement (after 3-5 clients)

**Channel 4: Partnerships (later)**
- Web designers → refer clients back and forth
- Google Ads freelancers → white-label GBP for their clients
- Marketing agencies → subcontract the GBP work for $250/client

### The 48-hour pilot close

The single most effective sales tool: **a free 5-minute Loom audit of their GBP.** Shows up in your cold outreach, your walk-in, your referrals. Once they watch it, 30-50% become clients.

## Part 5: Operations & Delivery

### The delivery workflow

**Onboarding (week 1, ~3 hours total per client):**
1. Contract signed via Stripe payment link
2. Welcome email with "what to expect" + photo request
3. GBP access request (manager or owner access)
4. Discovery call (30 min) — learn business, services, voice, target customers
5. Initial audit + optimization (see `03-DELIVERY-PLAYBOOK.md` for checklist)
6. Day 7: First post + first review response + welcome packet delivered

**Weekly (30-45 min per client):**
- Monday: Review last week's metrics (15 min)
- Tuesday/Thursday: Draft week's post + review responses (15 min)
- Friday: Schedule posts, send client update, queue next week (10 min)

**Monthly (1 hour per client):**
- Pull GBP Insights (calls, direction requests, searches, views)
- Generate monthly report (AI-assisted)
- Send to client + 30-min call or Loom
- Review goals, plan next month

**Quarterly (2 hours per client):**
- Competitor analysis
- Strategy review
- Renewal check-in (always month 9)

### The tool stack (full)

**Phase 1 (months 1-3, under 5 clients):**
- Free: GBP dashboard, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Loom, Stripe, ChatGPT/Claude
- Cost: $0-30/mo

**Phase 2 (months 4-9, 5-15 clients):**
- Localith: $29/mo (bulk GBP, AI review reply, heatmaps)
- BrightLocal: $39/mo (citation tracking, audits)
- Canva Pro: $13/mo (post graphics)
- Instantly.ai: $30/mo (cold email)
- Apollo.io: free tier (lead scraping)
- Cost: ~$110/mo

**Phase 3 (months 10+, 15+ clients):**
- Same as Phase 2 + VA wages ($800-1,500/mo)
- Cost: ~$1,000-1,700/mo (at 20 clients)

### Hiring plan

**Don't hire until you hit 20 clients.** The work doesn't scale linearly, but the *coordination cost* of a VA does. Until you have 20+ clients, you save more by being efficient than by delegating.

When you do hire:
- **First hire:** Part-time VA (15-20 hrs/wk) at $10-15/hr from the Philippines or Latin America
- **Tasks:** Drafting post copy, uploading photos, scheduling, first-pass review responses
- **You still:** Sales, final approval on all responses, client calls, strategy
- **Where to find:** OnlineJobs.ph, Belay, or direct outreach to local colleges
- **Effective rate after hire:** $150-300/hr of founder time (much better)

**Second hire (at 40+ clients):** Full-time ops manager who runs delivery, you focus on sales + strategy.

### AI augmentation (the real moat)

The single biggest competitive advantage: **using AI to do in 30 minutes what agencies do in 3 hours.**

Every part of delivery is AI-augmentable:
- **Review responses:** Claude/ChatGPT drafts, you review + personalize (5 min per batch)
- **Post copy:** AI writes 4 weeks of posts in 15 min based on business + season
- **Monthly reports:** Template + AI populates from GBP Insights export
- **Audits:** Loom script drafted by AI, you record and personalize
- **Onboarding:** AI-generated welcome packet from a 5-question intake

The full prompts are in `06-AI-PROMPTS.md`. The goal: **70% AI-drafted, 30% human-judgment.** That's how you scale to 20-30 clients without burning out.

## Part 6: Growth Roadmap

### 12-month milestone plan

| Month | Clients | MRR | Key milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2-3 | $1,000-1,500 | First clients via walk-ins + Loom audits |
| 2 | 4-5 | $2,000-2,500 | Cold email system live, audit Loom template optimized |
| 3 | 6-8 | $3,000-4,000 | First case study published, referral system set up |
| 4 | 8-10 | $4,000-5,000 | Localith + BrightLocal added, processes formalized |
| 6 | 12-15 | $6,000-7,500 | Cash flow covers all tools + buffer, partner referrals starting |
| 9 | 18-22 | $9,000-11,000 | **Hiring threshold** — first VA |
| 12 | 22-30 | $11,000-15,000 | VA onboarded, you back to sales-only; consider Pro tier upsell campaign |

### The 3 decision points (where you re-evaluate)

**Decision 1 (month 3):** Is the close rate >5% on cold outreach? If yes, scale channels. If no, refine offer or change niche.

**Decision 2 (month 6):** Is MRR >$5K with >70% retention? If yes, lean in. If no, either raise prices (you may be undercharging) or add Pro tier.

**Decision 3 (month 12):** Three paths forward:
- **(a) Keep going solo.** Cap at ~30 clients, $15K/mo, lifestyle business. Take on no new clients for 6 months if you're at capacity.
- **(b) Scale up.** Hire 1-2 VAs, then an ops manager. Target 50-80 clients, $30-50K/mo.
- **(c) Productize.** Build a SaaS that does the GBP management for $99/mo. Compete with Localith. Riskier, but bigger upside.
- **(d) Sell.** An agency book of 30 clients at $500-1K/mo sells for 2-3x annual revenue = $360-720K. If you want to exit, this is the path.

### What "success" looks like at 12 months

**Minimum viable success (MVS):** 10 clients, $5K MRR, run on the side, $5K/mo saved/invested.

**Solid success:** 20 clients, $10K MRR, hired first VA, considering full-time.

**Exceptional success:** 30+ clients, $15K+ MRR, systemized, options open for productization or exit.

## Part 7: Risk Register

See `07-RISK-AND-PIVOT.md` for the full register. Top 5 risks:

1. **Slow start (most common failure)** — 90 days of zero clients. Mitigation: walk-ins + Loom audits from week 1, not week 5.
2. **Churn spikes at month 3-4** — clients don't see enough lift, quit. Mitigation: clear expectations set at sale, monthly reporting from day 1.
3. **Founder burnout** — repetitive work + 60+ hr weeks. Mitigation: AI augmentation from day 1, processes documented, VA at client #20.
4. **Side-business conflict with Inspire11** — contract review needed. Mitigation: avoid Inspire11 clients, keep hours <20/wk, check employment agreement.
5. **Google algorithm change** — GBP becomes less important. Mitigation: most of the work (reviews, photos, content) helps SEO broadly, not just GBP.

## The honest conclusion

This business is not glamorous. It's a service business. You'll do the same kinds of tasks 200 times. You'll talk to local business owners who think "Google" is something they hire a nephew for.

But it works. The economics are real. The founder advantages you have are real. The market gap is real. The AI augmentation makes the margins real. And the optionality at 12 months (sell, scale, productize, keep) is the kind most people never get.

The question isn't whether this business can make money. It clearly can. The question is whether **you** will do the unsexy work for 6-12 months while everyone else is trying to be an AI unicorn.

If yes, this is a $5-15K/mo business within a year, with multiple paths from there.
