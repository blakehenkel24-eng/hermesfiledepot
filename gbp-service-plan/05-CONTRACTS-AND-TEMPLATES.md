# 05 — Contracts, Templates, and Sales Collateral

## 1. The Service Agreement (Master Contract)

Save as a Google Doc or PDF template. Send via DocuSign, HelloSign, or even just Gmail with a "please reply with 'I agree' if these terms work" — the latter is fine for month-to-month service agreements.

---

### **LOCAL LEAD ENGINE — SERVICE AGREEMENT**

**Effective Date:** [Date]

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

**Provider:** [Your Legal Name / Your LLC Name], ("Provider", "we", "us")
**Client:** [Client Business Legal Name] ("Client", "you")

---

**1. SERVICES**

Provider will deliver the following services for Client's Google Business Profile ("GBP"):

**Selected Tier:** ☐ Starter ($300/mo)  ☐ Growth ($500/mo)  ☐ Pro ($1,000/mo)

**Deliverables by tier:**

**Starter:**
- Initial GBP audit + optimization (one-time setup)
- 2 Google Posts per month
- Review response (every review, AI-drafted, Provider-approved)
- Monthly summary report
- Email support

**Growth:** All Starter deliverables, plus:
- 4 Google Posts per month
- 2-3 photo uploads per month
- Q&A management
- Quarterly competitor analysis
- 30-min monthly call or Loom
- Priority email support

**Pro:** All Growth deliverables, plus:
- 8 Google Posts per month
- 4-6 photo uploads per month
- Citation cleanup + 2 new citations per month
- Review generation campaign
- Biweekly 30-min strategy call
- Local SEO on-page optimization
- Same-day email support SLA

---

**2. TERM AND TERMINATION**

- This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues month-to-month.
- Either party may terminate with **30 days written notice** via email.
- Upon termination, Provider will deliver all account credentials and final reports within 7 days.
- No refunds for partial months.

---

**3. FEES AND PAYMENT**

- **Monthly recurring fee:** $[Amount] USD
- **One-time setup fee:** $[Amount] USD (waived for [N] pilot clients)
- **Payment method:** Stripe auto-debit on the 1st of each month
- **Late payment:** 7-day grace period, then 1.5% monthly interest
- **Annual prepay discount:** 10% off if paid upfront (12 months)

**First month + setup fee** are due upon signing.

---

**4. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES**

Client agrees to:
- Grant Provider Manager or Owner access to the GBP
- Provide business photos, logos, and brand assets within 14 days of signing
- Respond to Provider's questions within 5 business days
- Notify Provider of any business changes (hours, services, address, phone)
- Review and approve all deliverables within 7 days of receipt

---

**5. PROVIDER RESPONSIBILITIES**

Provider will:
- Deliver all services described in Section 1
- Maintain confidentiality of Client's business information
- Respond to Client communications within 1 business day
- Send a monthly report by the 5th of each month
- Maintain professional standards in all public-facing content

---

**6. SERVICE GUARANTEE**

If, after 90 days of continuous service, Client has not seen:
- 25% increase in GBP profile views, OR
- 10% increase in GBP calls or direction requests

(measured against the 30-day baseline before service start)

...Provider will continue working at no charge until either threshold is met, or for a maximum of 60 additional days, whichever comes first.

This guarantee requires Client to fulfill all Client Responsibilities in Section 4.

---

**7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

- Client owns all GBP content created by Provider (posts, photos, descriptions, Q&A)
- Provider retains the right to use anonymized case study data (e.g., "Client X increased calls by 35% in 6 months")
- Client may revoke the case study right in writing

---

**8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**

Provider's liability is limited to the fees paid in the 3 months preceding any claim. Provider is not liable for:
- Changes to Google's algorithm or GBP features
- Client's business performance changes unrelated to GBP
- Third-party actions (e.g., Google's review removal policies)
- Lost profits or consequential damages

---

**9. CONFIDENTIALITY**

Both parties agree to keep confidential any non-public business information shared during the engagement.

---

**10. GOVERNING LAW**

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. Disputes will be resolved in Cook County, Illinois courts.

---

**11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT**

This document is the entire agreement. Modifications must be in writing and signed by both parties.

---

Provider: _________________________ Date: _________

Client: _________________________ Date: _________

## 6. The Cancellation / Save Flow

**Email 1: "We've noticed..." (when churn signals appear)**

```
Subject: Quick check-in

Hi [Name],

Haven't heard from you in a bit and just wanted to check in.

A few questions:
1. How are you feeling about the work we're doing?
2. Are the monthly reports useful?
3. Anything I should be doing differently?

If there's something not working, I'd rather know now
than keep delivering work you're not seeing value in.

[Name]
```

**Email 2: "Saving the relationship" (if they say they're leaving)**

```
Subject: Before you go — 2 questions

Hi [Name],

Sorry to hear you're thinking of pausing. Before we wrap:

1. What's the main reason? (price / not seeing results /
   doing it yourself / going with someone else)
2. Is there anything I could change that would make you
   stay?

Sometimes a quick call clears up misunderstandings. If
you're open to it, here's my Calendly for the next 7 days:
[link]

so your service continues through [date + 30 days].
No hard feelings — I want this to work for you, not
be a sunk cost.

[Name]
```

Thanks for trying Local Lead Engine. Wishing you and
[Business] the best.

[Name]

## 9. The "If They Ask" Cheat Sheet

| They ask | You say |
|---|---|
| "How long have you been doing this?" | "I started [month]. I've been working with [niche] practices for [N] months and have [N] active clients." (Be honest if it's new.) |
| "Can I see case studies?" | "Yes — happy to share. Here are 2 recent wins: [metric 1] and [metric 2]." (If you have none yet, do your first 1-2 clients at 50% off in exchange for case study rights.) |
| "What if Google changes the algorithm?" | "Most of what I do (reviews, photos, posts, Q&A) helps your broader local search presence, not just GBP. Algorithm changes usually favor consistent, active profiles — which is exactly what I do for you." |
| "What happens to my account if you disappear?" | "You own everything. Posts, photos, Q&A — all on your GBP. I'd hand off the access to you in 24 hours if anything happened." |
| "I want a discount." | "I don't discount, but I have a Starter tier at $300/mo that's a lighter version. Want to see what's in that?" |
