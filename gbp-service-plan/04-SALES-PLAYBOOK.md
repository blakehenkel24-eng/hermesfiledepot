# 04 — Sales Playbook: From Zero to 20 Clients

The complete go-to-market motion. Channels, scripts, objection handlers, the 48-hour pilot close.

---

## 1. The Mental Model

Sales for this business is not a "closing" motion. It's a **diagnostic motion**. You walk in (or call in) and reveal something the prospect doesn't know about their own business. The close happens in their head, not in the pitch.

The single most effective weapon: **a personalized Loom audit video showing them what's wrong with their GBP, what their competitors are doing, and what you'd fix in month one.** This does 80% of the selling for you.

---

## 2. The 4 Channels (Ranked by Effectiveness)

### Channel 1: Door-to-Door Walk-Ins (First 5 clients)

**Best for:** Building confidence, getting first case studies, learning the pitch live.

**Targets (Schaumburg + adjacent suburbs):**
- Dental practices: ~50 in 5-mile radius
- Medspas: ~20-30
- Chiropractors: ~30-40
- Personal injury attorneys: ~15-25
- HVAC companies: ~40
- Real estate teams: ~50+

**The walk-in script (3 minutes max):**

> "Hi, I'm [Name] — I work with [dental/medspa] practices in the area to make sure their Google Business Profile is doing its job. I noticed your listing has [specific observation — e.g., 12 reviews vs. your competitor's 87]. I put together a quick 5-min video walking through what I'd do if you were my client — no charge. Want me to send it over?"

If yes:
> "What's the best email? I'll have it in your inbox by tomorrow."

If hesitant:
> "Totally understand. Even if you don't want to work with me, the video will show you 3-4 things you can fix yourself in 10 minutes. Worth a look?"

**Conversion rate:** 5-15% on follow-up. So 50 walk-ins → 2-7 clients.

**Time budget:** 5-10 walk-ins per 2-hour Saturday session. 2 sessions/month = 10-20 walk-ins/month. 1-3 clients/month from this channel alone.

**What to bring:**
- 25-50 business cards
- 1-page printout (see `05-CONTRACTS-AND-TEMPLATES.md`)
- iPhone with GBP open to their business
- Confidence (faked is fine, becomes real by week 3)

**What to wear:** Business casual. Not a suit (you're 23, you look like a kid in a suit). Khakis + button-down. Polished but not stiff.

### Channel 2: Cold Email (Scaling Past 5 Clients)

**Best for:** Scaling to 15-30 clients without driving around all day.

**The stack:**
- **Apollo.io** (free tier) — find local businesses + decision-maker emails
- **Instantly.ai** ($30/mo) — send campaigns at scale, manage replies
- **Loom** (free) — record audit videos
- **Stripe Payment Links** — close without a call (for the brave)

**Prospect list build:**

In Apollo, search:
- Job titles: Owner, Founder, Marketing Director, Office Manager
- Company size: 1-10, 11-50
- Industry: Dental Offices, Medical Spas, Chiropractors, HVAC, etc.
- Location: Schaumburg, IL + 10 mile radius
- Keywords: "dental" "medspa" "chiropractor" "HVAC" "law firm"

Extract 100-200 prospects per niche per week. That's enough for 4-5 campaigns/month.

**The cold email (3-touch sequence):**

**Email 1 (the hook):**
```
Subject: [Business Name] — your GBP is missing 40 photos

Hi [First name],

I was looking at [Business Name]'s Google Business Profile this morning
while searching for [their service] in [city]. Two things jumped out:

1. You have [12] reviews — [Competitor] has 87. That's roughly 7x the
   social proof showing up before someone clicks.
2. You have [4] photos uploaded — [Competitor] has 48. Google weighs
   photo count heavily when ranking the map pack.

Both are fixable in 30 days. I do this for [niche] practices in
[city] — I run a 5-minute video audit for free if you want to see
what I'd change in your first month.

Worth a look?

[Name]
[Calendly link]
```

**Email 2 (3 days later, only if no reply):**
```
Subject: re: [Business Name] — your GBP is missing 40 photos

Hi [First name],

Quick bump — sent a note a few days ago about your Google listing.

If now's not the right time, no worries. If you want to see the
5-min audit video, just reply "send it" and I'll fire it over today.

[Name]
```

**Email 3 (7 days after Email 1, only if no reply):**
```
Subject: One free thing for [Business Name]

Hi [First name],

Last note from me. Three things you can do in 10 minutes to improve
your Google Business Profile right now:

1. Upload 10-15 photos (office, team, work samples)
2. Add your services with descriptions (Google weights this heavily)
3. Respond to your last 5 reviews if any are unanswered

If you want the full audit video or want help doing this every week,
you know where to find me.

[Name]
```

**Expected conversion:** 1-3% reply rate, 10-30% close on replies. 200 emails/month → 1-3 clients.

**Personalization tax:** The Email 1 is *highly* personalized (specific numbers about their business). This is non-negotiable. Generic templates get 0.1% reply rates. Personalized gets 1-3%. The 30-60 seconds you spend per email is the difference.

### Channel 3: Referral Network (Compounds Over 6-12 Months)

**The system:**

After every successful client month 2+, ask for referrals:

> "Glad the work is going well. Quick favor: do you know any other [dentists/medspa owners] in the area who'd benefit from this kind of service? If they sign up, I'll send you a $100 Amazon gift card as a thank you."

**The math:** 1 referral per 3 clients per year. At 10 active clients, you get 3-4 referrals/year. ~25% close rate. So 1 client/year from referrals. Modest, but compounds.

**Better: formal referral partnerships**

Partner with 2-3 complementary service providers in your area:
- Web designer
- Brand photographer
- Google Ads freelancer
- Social media manager

Cross-refer clients. They send you their clients who need GBP, you send them your clients who need websites/ads.

Set up: 10-15% referral fee either direction, or trade referrals at 0% fee.

### Channel 4: Inbound (Month 6+)

After 6 months, you should have 2-3 published case studies. Time to start inbound:

- **LinkedIn posts** (1-2/week): share quick wins, screenshots of client GBP improvements
- **Local business Facebook groups** (Schaumburg Chamber, etc.)
- **Short-form video** (TikTok/Reels): "I run a local SEO agency. Here's what I see in your GBP that you don't." 

Inbound is slow to build but compounds forever. 6-12 months in, you should be getting 1-2 inbound leads/month from your content.

## 3. The 48-Hour Pilot Close

This is the conversion machine. Used for warm leads (referrals, walk-ins, email replies).

### The pilot offer

> "Let me do a free 5-minute audit video of your Google Business Profile. I'll show you what I'd change in your first 30 days if you were my client. You can take the video and do the work yourself, or hire me, or ignore it. No catch. If you want to talk after, my calendar's open. Cool?"

### The deliverable (within 48 hours of the ask)

A 5-7 minute Loom video with this structure:

1. **Cold open (30 sec):** "Hey [Name], thanks for letting me take a look. I want to walk you through what I see in your listing and what I'd do in month one."
2. **Their current state (90 sec):** Screen-share their GBP. Point out the top 3 things wrong (missing photos, low reviews, unoptimized description, no posts, no Q&A).
3. **The competitor gap (90 sec):** Show the top 3 competitors in their area. Compare photo counts, review counts, post cadence. Be specific.
4. **What I'd do in month 1 (90 sec):** The exact changes. Show the audit checklist in action.
5. **The ROI (60 sec):** "If we move you from page 3 to the top 3 on the map pack for '[service] in [city]', based on average click-through rates, you should see [X] more calls/month. At your average customer value, that's [$Y] in new revenue. The service pays for itself in 1-2 new customers."
6. **The close (30 sec):** "If you want to move forward, here's my Calendly. The standard package is $500/mo, month-to-month, cancel anytime. I'd love to work with you."

### Why this works

- **Personalized** (shows you did real work)
- **Diagnostic** (they learn something new)
- **Risk-free** (no commitment to listen)
- **Demonstrates expertise** (you're literally showing them what you'd do)
- **Self-qualifies** (if they don't book after this, they weren't going to anyway)

**Close rate on this:** 30-50% of prospects who watch the full video. 2-3x the close rate of any cold pitch without it.

## 4. The Discovery Call (After They Watch the Audit)

30 minutes. Goal: confirm fit, set expectations, close.

### Script

> "Thanks for taking 30 minutes. I want to make sure we're a good fit and that I can actually deliver what you need. Three questions:
>
> 1. **What's your #1 goal for the next 6 months?** More calls? More direction requests? More bookings? More 5-star reviews? I want to know what success looks like for you.
>
> 2. **What have you tried before for your Google listing?** DIY? An agency? A nephew? (This tells me how much education they need.)
>
> 3. **Who else needs to be involved in the decision?** (Often: spouse, partner, marketing director. Get them on the call now, not after you've sold.)
>
> Then I walk them through:
> - The exact deliverable (audit, posts, review responses, monthly report)
> - The timeline (week 1 setup, week 2+ delivery)
> - The guarantee (90-day free work if no lift)
> - The price ($500/mo Growth, $300/mo Starter, $1,000/mo Pro)
> - The contract terms (month-to-month, 30-day notice)
>
> Close: 'Want to get started this week? I'll send a Stripe link right after this call.'"

### Objection handlers

**"I need to think about it."**
> "Totally. What specifically do you want to think through? Price, fit, or something else?" (Surface the real objection.)

**"It's more than I wanted to spend."**
> "What's your monthly customer acquisition budget right now? Because if you're spending $0, one new customer from Google pays for 6-12 months of this. The math is: [LTV] × [expected new customers] vs. [$500/mo]." (Make the ROI obvious.)

**"I want to talk to my partner/spouse."**
> "Great. Want to schedule a 15-min call with both of you? I can answer their questions directly." (Get the second decision-maker on a call.)

**"Can you do it for $200/mo?"**
> "I appreciate the budget consideration. The work I do takes 30-45 min/week, and at $200 I'd be working for less than minimum wage. The Growth tier at $500 is where I can deliver real results. If budget is the issue, the Starter tier at $300 might fit better — fewer posts, no monthly call, but the core GBP work." (Don't cave. Discounting feels easy, then you're the cheap agency.)

**"My nephew/daughter/marketing person already does this."**
> "Cool. How's that working out? Are they posting weekly, responding to reviews within 24 hours, sending you a monthly report with metrics? If yes, no need for me. If not, I'm essentially the upgrade — they get to focus on something else." (Don't attack the existing person. Position as the upgrade.)

**"I want to see results first before I commit."**
> "That's exactly why I do the free audit. After that, the 90-day guarantee covers you — if I don't deliver a measurable lift in your GBP metrics, I work free until I do. That's about as 'see results first' as you can get."

### Closing

After the call, send a Stripe payment link within 10 minutes (strike while the iron is hot). If they say "let me think about it," set a specific follow-up: "Cool, I'll follow up Friday at 3pm CT. If you decide before then, the link's in this email."

Don't chase. Set a follow-up, then let them come to you.

## 5. The Sales Pipeline (Manage This Weekly)

Use a simple Google Sheet or Notion board:

| Stage | Definition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | Identified business, not contacted | Add to outreach list |
| **Contacted** | First touch sent (walk-in, email) | Track response |
| **Engaged** | Replied, watched audit, or had call | Schedule next step |
| **Audit Sent** | Loom audit delivered | Wait for response, follow up in 3 days |
| **Discovery Booked** | Call scheduled | Prep with research |
| **Proposal Sent** | Stripe link sent | Wait 48 hours, follow up |
| **Won** | Payment received | Start onboarding |
| **Lost** | Said no | Note reason, move on (don't chase) |
| **Nurture** | Maybe later (3+ months) | Quarterly check-in email |

**Target numbers per month (steady state):**
- 50 new leads added
- 20 contacted
- 5 engaged
- 3 audits sent
- 2 discovery calls
- 1-2 won

This is the funnel. The math: 50 leads × 4% close = 2 clients/month. Track it weekly.

## 6. The First 30 Days Sales Plan

### Days 1-7: Setup
- Build your prospect list (100+ businesses in target niche)
- Record 1 sample Loom audit on a real local business (as practice)
- Print 50 business cards
- Print 25 of the 1-pager
- Set up Apollo.io, Instantly.ai, Calendly, Stripe

### Days 8-14: First outreach
- 5 walk-ins per day on Saturday (2 days)
- 20 cold emails per day (4 days)
- Send 1 follow-up to anyone who engaged

### Days 15-21: First sales
- 3-5 audits requested → deliver all within 48 hours
- 1-3 discovery calls booked
- First close attempt

### Days 22-30: First client + iteration
- 1-2 clients signed
- Onboarding in progress
- First week of delivery for new client
- Document what worked, what didn't
- Adjust pitch, audit template, email copy

**Day 30 target:** 1-2 paying clients, $500-1,000 MRR, 5-10 active leads in pipeline.

---

## 7. What "Good Pipeline Hygiene" Looks Like

- Every Monday: review the pipeline, follow up on stalled leads
- Every Friday: 5 new walk-ins OR 20 new cold emails (never zero outreach)
- Every week: at least 1 audit video in progress
- Every month: at least 1 discovery call booked for next month

The pipeline is a muscle. If you stop feeding it, it dies in 30 days. **Treat outreach like gym time — non-negotiable, scheduled in advance.**

---

## 8. The "I Have No Idea What to Say" Backup

If you freeze in a walk-in or on a call, this works:

> "I'm [Name]. I help [niche] businesses in [area] get found on Google. Two questions:
> 1. Are you happy with how many calls you're getting from your Google listing?
> 2. If I showed you a 5-minute video of what I'd change to get you more calls, would you watch it?"

Then just shut up and wait for the answer. They'll either say yes (and you send the audit) or no (and you thank them and leave). Either way, you've delivered value and not been salesy.
