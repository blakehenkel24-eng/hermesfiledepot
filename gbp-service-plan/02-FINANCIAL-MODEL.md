**The real constraint is not unit economics. It's founder hours.**

## 2. Founder Time Budget

Blake has, realistically, ~12-15 usable hours/week for this business:

## 4. Break-Even Analysis

**Cash break-even:** 1 client. The moment you have one $500/mo client, you cover all your tools.

**Time break-even:** 2-3 clients. Below that, you're investing more time than you're getting back. But the *option value* of those 2-3 clients (case studies, systems, social proof) is real.

**"Should I keep going" break-even:** ~5 clients or month 4. If you don't have 5 paying clients by month 4, something is wrong with your offer/channel — pivot or stop.

## 5. Year 2 Scenarios

At end of Year 1, you have a base of 15-30 clients, $7-15K MRR, validated systems, a VA optionally in place. Three paths:

**Path A: Lifestyle (cap at $15K/mo)**
- Stop acquiring new clients at 25-30
- Raise prices 20-30% (existing + new)
- Reduce work to 15-20 hrs/wk
- MRR: $15-20K, take-home: $13-17K/mo
- Value: optionality, low stress, side income

**Path B: Scale (target $30-50K/mo)**
- Hire ops manager ($5-6K/mo)
- Add Pro tier aggressively, add citation/SEO upsells
- Move to 2-3 VAs
- MRR: $30-50K, take-home: $20-35K/mo
- Value: real business, possibly full-time replacement

**Path C: Productize**
- Build SaaS that does GBP for $99-299/mo
- Compete with Localith, BrightLocal
- Requires $50-200K investment + 12-18 months
- Upside: $1-10M ARR exit potential
- Risk: high — most SaaS in this space will fail

**Path D: Sell**
- Position agency book of 25-50 clients
- Sell at 2-3x annual revenue = $300K-1.8M
- 3-6 month transition, then walk
- Best if you want to move on, take the cash, start something else

## 6. Sensitivity Analysis

| Variable | Change | Impact on Year 1 Net |
|---|---|---|
| Close rate on walk-ins | 5% → 10% | +$15K |
| Average client lifetime | 12 mo → 18 mo | +$20K (compounds) |
| Price point | $500 → $600 | +$14K (no volume loss expected) |
| Time to first client | 30 days → 60 days | -$3K (not huge) |
| Hire VA at month 6 instead of 9 | (no impact on Year 1) | -$4K (early wage cost) |
| Annual prepay discount (10%) taken by 30% of clients | Better cash flow | -$1.5K Year 1 (worth it for retention) |

**The biggest lever is retention.** A 12-month average client lifetime vs. 18-month = $60K difference over 5 years. Focus obsessively on monthly value delivery.

## 8. The "If I Were You" Math

If you (Blake) execute the base case:
- 12-month side income: $67K
- Hours invested: ~750
- Effective hourly: $90 (on the side of your $100K+ Inspire11 salary)
- Asset built: agency book worth $300-500K as an exit
- Options: 4 paths (lifestyle, scale, productize, sell)

**Risk-adjusted ROI:** This is one of the highest-expected-value side businesses available to a 23-year-old with strong business skills and AI fluency. The reason is the *optionality* — the worst case ($55K) is still great. The best case ($130K + sale) is life-changing.

**The one number to track weekly:** Active clients. If this is going up by 1-2/month, you're on track. If it's flat, something is broken.

## 9. The "What If" Calculator

Quick mental model for "is this month going well?":

- **Cash MRR:** X
- **Net new clients this month:** 1-2 (target)
- **Churn:** 0 (target)
- **Active deliverable per client:** 4+ posts/month, all reviews responded to, monthly report sent
- **Founder hours logged:** 12-15
- **New leads this week:** 5+

If all five of these are green, you're on the base-case trajectory. If two or more are red for two consecutive months, time to diagnose.
